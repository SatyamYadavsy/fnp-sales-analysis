# FNP Sales Analysis — Executive Summary

## Executive Overview

The FNP Sales Analysis project evaluates sales performance across revenue, orders, occasions, product categories, individual products, cities, order timing, monthly trends, and delivery performance.

The purpose of the analysis is to identify the major drivers of sales performance and highlight opportunities related to marketing, product strategy, seasonal planning, customer targeting, and delivery efficiency.

The project was developed in Microsoft Excel using Power Query, Power Pivot, PivotTables, calculated measures, slicers, timeline filters, and interactive charts.

---

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 1,000 |
| Total Revenue | ₹35,20,984 |
| Average Order Value | ₹3,521 |
| Average Order-to-Delivery Time | 5.53 days |
| Quantity vs. Delivery-Time Correlation | 0.00348 |

### Revenue Calculation

Revenue was calculated as:

**Revenue = Quantity × Price**

With total revenue of **₹35,20,984** across **1,000 orders**, the Average Order Value is approximately **₹3,521**.

# Correlation Analysis

A Pearson correlation analysis was performed between:

- **Quantity ordered**
- **Order-to-delivery duration**

The calculated correlation coefficient was:

**r = 0.00348**

This value is extremely close to zero.

## Interpretation

There is **virtually no linear relationship** between the quantity ordered and the number of days required to deliver an order.

In practical terms:

> Larger orders do not appear to take meaningfully longer to deliver than smaller orders.

This suggests that order quantity itself is not a major factor influencing delivery duration.

Other factors may have a greater impact, including:

- Delivery location
- Product category
- Product availability
- Seasonal demand
- Order date
- Shipping conditions
- Operational capacity

It is also important to note that correlation measures linear association and does not imply causation.

## 1. Strengthen Occasion-Based Marketing

Prioritize campaigns around high-revenue occasions such as:

- Anniversaries
- Raksha Bandhan
- Holi

These occasions already demonstrate strong customer demand and may provide opportunities for further revenue growth.

---

## 2. Promote High-Performing Categories

Increase visibility of strong-performing categories such as:

- Colors
- Soft Toys
- Sweets

These categories can be featured in recommendations, promotions, and product bundles.

---

## 3. Leverage Best-Selling Products

Top-performing products such as **Magnam Set** and **Quia Gift** can be used for:

- Cross-selling
- Bundling
- Featured recommendations
- Occasion-specific promotions

---

## 4. Plan for Seasonal Peaks

Use monthly revenue patterns to prepare inventory, marketing campaigns, and operational capacity ahead of high-demand periods.

---

## 5. Optimize Promotional Timing

Test targeted campaigns during evening hours when revenue activity appears to be strongest.

---

## 6. Use Regional Targeting

Use city-level order data to create more localized and relevant marketing campaigns.

---

## 7. Investigate Delivery Drivers

Since order quantity has almost no linear relationship with delivery duration, delivery optimization should focus on other operational variables.

Further analysis can investigate:

- City-wise delivery time
- Product-wise delivery time
- Category-wise delivery performance
- Seasonal delivery delays
- Product availability

The business generated **₹35.21 lakh in total revenue from 1,000 orders**, with an **Average Order Value of approximately ₹3,521**.

The analysis identifies several important business patterns:

- Sales are strongly influenced by gifting occasions.
- Selected product categories contribute disproportionately to revenue.
- Revenue shows clear seasonal variation.
- Evening hours show stronger customer activity.
- Sales are distributed across multiple geographic markets.
- Order quantity has virtually no linear relationship with delivery duration.

Overall, the project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights using data cleaning, modeling, KPI development, interactive analysis, and business interpretation.
